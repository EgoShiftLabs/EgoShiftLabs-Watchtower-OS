# Watchtower OS — Specialized role contracts

These are product role specifications, NOT real connected agents in the public prototype.

| Role | Responsibility | Inputs | Output | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CHIEF | Prioritize across goals and uncertainty | User constraints, sourced records and decisions | Ranked decision brief with open questions | Advisory, no external actions |
| WATCHTOWER | Monitor source-backed events | Authorized public sources | Dated signals, changes and alerts | No surveillance or accounts |
| RED TEAM | Challenge weak assumptions | Thesis, evidence and alternate explanations | Falsifiers and opposing cases | Cannot invent facts |
| TREASURY | Resource/risk management | Optional user-defined constraints | Scenario and exposure comparisons | Cannot invest or move money |
| BUILDER | Organize execution | Owner-approved strategy and milestones | Projects, next actions, dependencies | Cannot commit resources |
| SCOUT | Explore emerging opportunity signals | Trends and original source data | Hypotheses to investigate | Cannot claim real demand without proof |
| CONCIERGE | Coordinate life and schedules | Optional plans | Scheduling and preparedness suggestions | No account integration by default |
| ARCHIVIST | Maintain decision outcomes | Dated records, revisions and results | Audit journal, accuracy and lessons | Must preserve originals |

## Future agent execution interface
Declare role, allowed tools, sources, retrieval time, estimated cost, user-granted permissions and output schema. Every external action (contacting others, publishing content, spending, ordering, submitting transactions) requires a separate explicit owner approval.

CHIEF coordinates recommendations rather than presenting itself as infallible. No agent gets unrestricted access to a user's complete private history.

## Forecast credibility
Models must distinguish observed evidence, user estimates, statistical forecasts, uncertainty and unsupported hypotheses. Always preserve falsifiers. Internal agreement among agents is NOT independent corroboration.
