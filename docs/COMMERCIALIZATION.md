# Watchtower OS — Commercialization options and acquisition readiness

**Current stage: public prototype.** No paying customers, model accuracy, trained predictor, private dataset, secured backend, validated recurring revenue or actual acquisition offer is asserted.

## Target users to investigate
- People practicing rigorous personal forecasting and scenario planning.
- Independent researchers needing a falsifiable record of predictions and decisions.
- Business leaders seeking event-to-decision intelligence.
- Education and research organizations teaching probability, bias and source evaluation.
- Future specialist professional teams once authenticated security exists.

These are product hypotheses, not proven segments.

## Potential commercialization paths
1. Secure hosted subscription providing private planning and decision journal.
2. Licensed forecast journal and scoring component, with buyer-supplied legitimate datasets.
3. Research-team version with audited review and independent outcome resolution.
4. White-label product after private infrastructure, legal rights and sustainable customer support.

## Value drivers that would have to be earned
Validated forecasting improvements vs. baseline, retention, real user outcome evidence, reliable source provenance, secure private user data, low support costs, credible human-in-the-loop workflows and legitimate dataset licenses.

## Buyer-ready records
- Technical architecture, repository history, deployment documentation and tests.
- Author/contributor IP chain-of-title, AI-assisted development disclosures and dependency/data license records.
- Forecast model cards with out-of-sample evaluation, if actual models exist.
- Operational costs, customer acquisition/retention, margins, signed agreements, incident logs and privacy policies.
- Clear distinction between the public code license and privately owned secrets or datasets.
- Migration, account and service transition plan without violating user consent.

## Commercial caution
The name must be checked for trademark conflicts before investments in exclusive branding. Copyright does not grant exclusivity over a generic business idea, and publicly published code cannot simply be treated as a trade secret later. Avoid projecting an acquisition value without actual buyer demand or closed transaction comparables.
